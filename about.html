Our Story

Building Trust Since 1993

Gamage Builders was founded in 1993 with a clear mission: deliver dependable construction services to communities and clients across Sri Lanka. Over the decades, we have expanded our capabilities while staying rooted in quality, safety, and honest dealings.

Today we are recognised for road construction, building works, water and environmental infrastructure, machinery supply, and a full range of related civil engineering services — serving government agencies, private developers, and local communities.
